# OM Powerhouse

Build a premium, cinematic homepage for OM Solutions (Indian clean-energy & engineering company, dual fuel kit technology) inspired by CATAGEN's visual storytelling and industrial-technology aesthetic.

Key requirements:
1. Full-Screen Cinematic Hero:
   - Full viewport, autoplaying, looping, muted video with fallback poster image (/videos/om-solutions-hero.mp4) and dark cinematic overlay.
   - Large typography: "POWERING A CLEANER INDIA, ONE ENGINE AT A TIME."
   - Subtitle: "Smarter Power. Lower Fuel Cost. Cleaner Performance."
   - CTAs: "EXPLORE OUR TECHNOLOGY →" and "SEE OUR IMPACT ↓".
   - Animated scroll indicator: "SCROLL TO EXPLORE ↓".
2. Navigation:
   - Transparent floating header that transitions to sticky dark blurred on scroll.
   - Links: Home, Technology, Applications, Installations, Gallery, News, Contact + "GET IN TOUCH" primary button. Mobile responsive hamburger menu.
3. The Problem & Mission:
   - "INDIA DOESN'T NEED TO REPLACE EVERY ENGINE."
   - "RETROFIT. TRANSFORM. RUN CLEANER." with clean industrial aesthetic.
4. Product Flow & How It Works:
   - "FROM DIESEL TO DUAL FUEL" visual transformation pipeline (Diesel Engine → OM Solutions Dual Fuel Kit → Diesel + Gas → Smarter, Cleaner Operation).
   - "A SMARTER WAY TO POWER EXISTING ENGINES" explaining controlled gaseous fuel injection, regulation, mixing, engine combustion, and sensor monitoring.
5. Why Dual Fuel Benefits:
   - Up to 70% gaseous fuel potential, Lower operating fuel cost, No engine replacement, Same power level, Dual mode flexibility (100% diesel or dual fuel), Quick installation, Lower maintenance, Reduced particulate emissions.
6. Technology Schematic & Interactive Hotspots:
   - Visual engine/kit schematic with interactive hotspots for: Gas Air Mixer, Gas Flow Control Valve, Gas Pressure Regulator, Gas Filter, Electronic Gas Shut-off Valve, Gas Pressure Sensor, Knock Sensor, Exhaust Gas Temperature Sensor, Engine RPM Sensor, Control Panel.
7. Applications Grid:
   - Generators, Trucks & Buses, Tractors, Earth Movers, Borewells, Air Compressors, Marine Propulsion, Industrial Applications with rich imagery and hover effects.
8. Real-World Installations & Verified Data:
   - Case study cards featuring verified data from the presentation:
     * KOEL 320 kVA (Diesel + PNG, 65% Diesel Replacement, 34% Reported Fuel-Cost Saving)
     * Birla Tisya (Bengaluru • May 2026)
     * Nevatia Steel & Alloys (Dual Fuel Controller, sensors, gas system)
9. Cleaner India & Field Gallery:
   - "EVERY ENGINE COUNTS." and "FROM THE FIELD" photo/installation showcase.
10. Final CTA & Comprehensive Footer:
    - "READY TO POWER A CLEANER FUTURE?" → "TALK TO OM SOLUTIONS →".
    - Footer with company background (Est. 2021 by Prasad Parulekar, IIT Bombay / ex-Cummins), contact details (omsolns18@gmail.com, +91 9552545042, Pune, Maharashtra, India 411058), and navigation.
11. Design Language:
    - Deep forest greens (#0A1F18 / #0D281E), dark charcoal/slate (#0F172A), crisp whites, accents of vibrant clean-energy green (#10B981 / #00E599), smooth animations, responsive across mobile, tablet, and desktop.
